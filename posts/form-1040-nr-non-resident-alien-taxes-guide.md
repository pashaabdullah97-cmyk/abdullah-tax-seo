# Form 1040-NR Non-Resident Alien US Tax Return Checklist (2026-11-26)

## Overview
As a non-resident alien with US source income, you must file a US tax return using Form 1040-NR. This guide provides a comprehensive checklist for preparing your Form 1040-NR tax return for the year 2025, which will be filed in 2026.

## Key Points
- **Tax Year**: 2025 returns filed in 2026
- **Deadline**: April 15, 2026 (or June 15, 2026 if you file for an extension)
- **Required Forms**: Form 1040-NR, W-2 form, and any other relevant forms

## Practical Checklist
### Step 1: Gather Necessary Forms
- **Form 1040-NR**: This is the primary form for non-resident aliens.
- **W-2 Form**: If you received wages subject to US income tax withholding, you will need to attach your W-2 form.
- **Other Forms**: Depending on your income sources, you may need to attach additional forms such as Form 1099-MISC for non-employee compensation.

### Step 2: Calculate Your Income
- **Gross Income**: Sum up all your income sources, including wages, dividends, interest, and any other sources.
- **Taxable Income**: Subtract any allowable deductions from your gross income to determine your taxable income.

### Step 3: Determine Your Tax Liability
- **Taxable Income**: Use the IRS withholding tables or a tax calculator to determine your tax liability.
- **Tax Credits**: Claim any available tax credits such as the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE) or the Foreign Tax Credit.

### Step 4: Prepare Your Tax Return
- **Form 1040-NR Instructions**: Review the instructions provided with Form 1040-NR.
- **Form 1040-NR Worksheet**: Complete the worksheet to calculate your tax liability.
- **Form 1040-NR Schedule**: Fill out the Schedule to summarize your income and deductions.
