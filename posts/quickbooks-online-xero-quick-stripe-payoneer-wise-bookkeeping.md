What is QuickBooks Online and Xero?
===
QuickBooks Online and Xero are popular accounting software that provide comprehensive bookkeeping services for e-commerce and remote businesses. QuickBooks Online integrates seamlessly with Stripe, Payoneer, and Wise, making it a popular choice for businesses needing audit-ready books. Xero, on the other hand, offers similar features and integrates with these platforms as well.
===
QuickBooks Online & Xero Integration with Stripe
===
QuickBooks Online and Xero integrate with Stripe through Connect, allowing businesses to process and track payments in real-time. This integration simplifies bookkeeping and ensures that all transactions are accurately recorded. To get started, follow these steps:
1. Navigate to Connect in QuickBooks Online.
2. Search for Stripe and follow the integration steps.
3. Use Xero’s Payoneer integration to automatically sync transactions.
===
Xero Integration with Payoneer and Wise
===
Xero also integrates with Payoneer and Wise, making it easier to manage these payment methods. To set up the integration:
1. Navigate to Payoneer in Xero’s payment methods section.
2. Follow the instructions to connect your Payoneer account.
3. Connect your Wise accounts in Xero’s payment methods section.
===
Resolving Discrepancies in QuickBooks Online
===
Discrepancies can occur due to manual errors or missed transactions. To resolve discrepancies, follow these steps:
1. Review transactions in QuickBooks Online and Xero.
2. Update journal entries and adjust accruals and deferrals as necessary.
3. Use QuickBooks Online’s reconciliation features to match transactions accurately.
