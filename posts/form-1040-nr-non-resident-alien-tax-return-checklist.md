### Introduction
Form 1040-NR is a crucial tax return for non-resident aliens who have US source income. This guide will help you navigate the process, including due dates, filing requirements, and necessary forms. For more detailed information, refer to the IRS instructions for Form 1040-NR.

### Who Needs to File Form 1040-NR?
Nonresident aliens who are required to file under the Form 1040-NR filing rules, including those with effectively connected inclusions in the US, must file Form 1040-NR. If you received US wages subject to income tax withholding or had an office or place of business in the US, you must file by April 15, 2026. If you don't meet these criteria, the deadline is June 15, 2026.
