# US LLC Form 5472 and Form 1120: Foreign Owned Filing Guide

## Overview
As a non-US founder with a single-member US LLC, it is crucial to understand the filing requirements for Form 5472 and Form 1120. This guide will help you navigate the compliance requirements and avoid penalties.

## Key Points
- **Form 5472**: Filed annually by a US corporation that is at least 25% foreign-owned and has had at least one reportable transaction with a related party during the tax year. Single-member LLCs must also file Form 5472.
- **Form 1120**: Used by foreign-owned single-member LLCs to report their income to the IRS.
- **Penalties**: Failure to file results in a $25,000 penalty per form per year, with additional penalties of $25,000 every 30 days after an IRS notice if not filed.

## Checklist
To ensure compliance, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Determine Foreign Ownership
- **Form 5472**: Filed by a US corporation that is at least 25% foreign-owned and has had at least one reportable transaction with a related party during the tax year.
- **Single-member LLCs**: Must file Form 5472 if you own 25% or more of the LLC.

### Step 2: File Form 5472
- **Filing Date**: April 15, 2026, for the 2025 tax year.
- **Penalties**: $25,000 penalty per form per year, with additional penalties of $25,000 every 30 days after an IRS notice if not filed.

### Step 3: File Form 1120
- **Filing Date**: April 15, 2026, for the 2025 tax year.
- **Penalties**: $25,000 penalty per form per year, with additional penalties of $25,000 every 30 days after an IRS notice if not filed.
