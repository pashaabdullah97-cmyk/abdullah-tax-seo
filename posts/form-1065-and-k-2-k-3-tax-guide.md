## Form 1065 & K-2/K-3 Tax Guide: Simplifying Partnership Taxes for Multi-member US LLCs

In 2026, multi-member US LLCs and partnerships are required to file Form 1065, U.S. Return of Partnership Income, to report income and expenses. Understanding the intricacies of Form 1065 and its related schedules, such as K-2 and K-3, is crucial for ensuring compliance with IRS requirements. This guide will help you navigate the complexities of these forms and schedules.

### Who is Required to File K-2 and K-3?

Partnerships are exempt from completing Schedules K-2 and K-3 if they meet four specific conditions:
1. Total receipts for the tax year were less than $250,000.
2. Total assets at the end of the tax year were less than $1 million.
3. Schedules K-1 are filed with the return and furnished to the partners on or before the due date for the partnership return.
4. The partnership is not filing and is not required to file Schedule M-3.

### Understanding the Difference Between K-2 and K-3

- **K-2**: Used for domestic partnerships.
- **K-3**: Used for foreign partnerships.

### Filing Schedule K-1 with Form 1065

Yes, Schedule K-1 must be filed with Form 1065. Schedule K-1 details the income, deductions, credits, and other information that partners are entitled to receive from the partnership.

### Purpose of Schedule K-2

Schedule K-2 serves as a record of the partnership's financial status, including total receipts, total assets at the end of the tax year, and any other information required by the IRS.
