## US LLC Form 5472 and Form 1120 Foreign Owned Filing Guide

### Overview
As a non-US founder with a single-member US LLC, you need to understand the filing requirements for Form 5472 and Form 1120. Form 5472 is a key form for reporting foreign-owned US corporations, while Form 1120 is the main tax return for US corporations. This guide will help you navigate these requirements and avoid penalties.

### Key Points to Know

#### Filing Requirements
- **Form 5472**: This form must be filed by the Form 1120 due date. For calendar-year LLCs, this is April 15, extendable to October 15 with Form 7004. For tax year 2026, the Form 1120 due date is April 15.
- **Form 1120**: This is the main tax return for the LLC. If you are a foreign-owned single-member LLC, you may need to file a pro forma Form 1120 with Form 5472 attached.

#### Penalties
- **Initial Penalty**: $25,000 per form for failure to file.
- **Continuation Penalties**: Additional $25,000 penalties for each 30-day period after 90 days from an IRS notice.
- **Maximum Penalties**: There is no cap on total penalties, and if an LLC never filed, it could face six-figure penalties regardless of business size.

### FAQ
1. **What is the penalty for not filing Form 5472?**
   - The initial penalty is $25,000 per form, with additional $25,000 penalties for each 30-day period after 90 days from an IRS notice.
2. **Does my LLC need to file Form 5472?**
   - Yes, if you are a foreign-owned single-member LLC, you must file Form 5472 with a pro forma Form 1120.
3. **What is the late filing penalty for Form 1120?**
   - There is no specific late filing penalty for Form 1120. However, failure to file Form 5472 can lead to penalties.
4. **What is the penalty for late filing my LLC taxes with the IRS?**
   - Penalties can be severe, including $25,000 per form for failure to file and additional penalties for each 30-day period after 90 days from an IRS notice.
